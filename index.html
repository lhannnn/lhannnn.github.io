My research explores two closely connected directions:

Reliable Agents
  1. Knowing when to act and when to abstain. Agentic Abstention · HERA
  2. Maintaining behavioral consistency and evaluating safety in agent interactions. SPASM · DialogGuard
  3. Transferring learned experience to open-world embodied navigation. SAGE
Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI)
  1. Evolving agent context through lessons learned from interaction trajectories. CONVOLVE
  2. Co-evolving agent harnesses and environments through execution feedback. HERA
